The "Big Three" Shortcut Formulas
1. Difference of Squares
\( a^2 - b^2 = (a - b)(a + b) \)
2. Perfect Square (Sum)
\( a^2 + 2ab + b^2 = (a + b)^2 \)
3. Perfect Square (Diff)
\( a^2 - 2ab + b^2 = (a - b)^2 \)
